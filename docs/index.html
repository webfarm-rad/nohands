The math

Takes from traders. Gives to holders.

Every $ROBOHOOD trade on Pons pays a 1% fee. Pons lets the creator route their share of that fee to holders instead of themselves. Robo Hood turned that switch on at launch. It cannot be turned off.

1.00%fee on every buy and sell

  • SUPPLY1,000,000,000, fixed. Pons tokens cannot be minted after launch.
  • LIQUIDITYLocked permanently by the Pons factory at launch. Not "locked for 6 months". Forever.
  • DEV BUYDeclared before launch in the runbook and on X. Whatever the human bought at launch is public onchain.
  • GRADUATIONThe curve graduates into a locked Uniswap pool once 4.2 ETH is raised. Same rules as every Pons v2 token.
  • WHAT THIS IS NOTNot a security, not a fund, not a yield product. A memecoin whose fee routing is set to "holders". That's it.
How to buy

Three steps, ~0.5 second finality.

  1. Get ETH on Robinhood Chain. Bridge from Ethereum, Arbitrum or Base at docs.robinhood.com/chain, or use any bridge that lists Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663). Gas is paid in ETH and costs fractions of a cent.
  2. Open the $ROBOHOOD page on Pons. Use the button at the top of this page. Check that the contract address matches the one shown here. Copycats will exist within minutes of launch.
  3. Buy, hold, claim. Your share of trading fees accrues automatically. Claim it any time from your profile menu on Pons.
